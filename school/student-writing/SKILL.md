description: Write or revise essay using an undergraduate student voice.
---

## Voice

- Write like a second-year student explaining what they understand. Use familiar words, straightforward grammar and short, connected sentences. Avoid the tone of an expert research paper or textbook.
- Be natural and direct. Remove polished filler, unnecessary jargon and complicated phrasing. Do not deliberately introduce mistakes to sound like a student.
- Use simple vocabulary, straightforward grammar, and reasonably not too long sentences. Minor grammatical imperfections are acceptable. Avoid overly polished or sophisticated language.

## Reasoning and emphasis

- Start from relevant lecture ideas and use them for the explaination. Make the connection through the idea relationship, not just a citation or concept name
